Privacy Policy - Cool Calm Uploader

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Cool Calm Uploader ("the app") is a private tool run by the owner of the YouTube channel Cool Calm Vibes. It runs on the owner's own computer and uploads videos to that one channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Google and YouTube data the app uses

The app uses the YouTube API Services. When the channel owner signs in, the app asks Google for:

  • youtube.upload - to upload videos, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and publish schedules to the owner's channel.
  • youtube.readonly - to read the name of the signed-in channel, so the app can refuse to upload to any channel other than Cool Calm Vibes.

The app does not read, collect or store any data about viewers, subscribers, comments or other YouTube users.

How data is stored

The sign-in token Google issues is stored only in a file on the owner's own computer. The app keeps a local list of the videos it has uploaded (video ID, title and publish time). Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's.

Sharing

The app does not share, sell or transfer any data to anyone. No analytics, advertising or tracking services are used.

Data retention and deletion

The app stores no YouTube API data other than the IDs, titles and publish times of the owner's own uploads, which are kept only as long as the videos exist on the channel. To delete everything the app holds, delete the token.json and uploaded.json files from the app folder, or uninstall the app by deleting the folder. Revoking access (below) also makes Google's sign-in token stop working immediately. A deletion request sent to the contact address below is completed within 7 days.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from your Google Account's third-party access settings. Deleting the token file on the computer also stops the app from working until it is signed in again.

YouTube and Google terms

By using this app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
